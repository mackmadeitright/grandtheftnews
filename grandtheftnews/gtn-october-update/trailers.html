The Vice City Dispatch

GTA 6 Trailers

Every official Grand Theft Auto VI reveal Rockstar has released — two record-smashing trailers and the 27-minute Extended Look that finally showed gameplay.

The Extended Look — August 2026 (newest)

On August 27, 2026, Rockstar released Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look — a roughly 27-minute feature that premiered exclusively on Netflix for six hours before landing on the Rockstar Games YouTube channel. After two cinematic trailers and more than 18 months without new official footage, this was the first extended look at actual gameplay, captured entirely from the PS5 version. Rather than a mission-by-mission breakdown, it played like slices of a film — leaning into atmosphere, the relationship between protagonists Jason and Lucia, and a tour of Vice City and the wider state of Leonida, while touching on systems like the Wanted system, character customization, and the open world.

▶ Watch the Extended Look on Rockstar's official YouTube

Trailer 2 — May 2025

Rockstar surprise-dropped the second trailer in May 2025, just days after announcing a delay. Captured on PlayStation 5, it gave a fuller look at Vice City and the surrounding state of Leonida, fleshed out the Jason-and-Lucia story, and showed off the game's environments and cast. It broke records as one of the biggest video launches ever, pulling hundreds of millions of views across platforms in its first 24 hours — though, like the first, it stayed cinematic rather than showing raw gameplay.

▶ Watch Trailer 2 on Rockstar's official YouTube

Trailer 1 — December 2023

Grand Theft Auto VI — Trailer 1 (Rockstar Games, official)

The first official trailer arrived in December 2023 — actually a day early, after it leaked online and Rockstar pushed it out ahead of schedule. Set to Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road," it confirmed the long-rumored return to Vice City, the modern-day setting, and our first look at protagonists Lucia and Jason. It immediately became the most-viewed video game reveal trailer in YouTube history, drawing over 90 million views in 24 hours and setting multiple Guinness World Records.

Was the Extended Look "Trailer 3"?

Not officially — Rockstar branded it as "An Extended Look" rather than a numbered trailer, but in practice it filled the role fans expected a third trailer to play: the first real gameplay reveal ahead of launch. Rockstar has not announced a separate, traditional Trailer 3, and with the November 19, 2026 release date locked in, the marketing push has shifted toward a steady drip of screenshots, official details, and features like the Extended Look. Any further official video will hit the news wire the moment it drops.

The records, at a glance

  • Trailer 1 (Dec 2023): 90M+ YouTube views in 24 hours — the most-viewed game reveal trailer ever, with multiple Guinness World Records.
  • Trailer 2 (May 2025): hundreds of millions of views across platforms in 24 hours — among the biggest video launches of all time.
  • Extended Look (Aug 2026): the first official gameplay footage, a Netflix-exclusive premiere before going free on YouTube — a first-of-its-kind rollout for the industry.
